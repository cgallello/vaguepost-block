Runs on your device

Classification uses Chrome's built-in Gemini Nano when available. VagueBlock has no cloud model, analytics, ad network, or telemetry.

Your rules

Choose Review-only, Blur & Review, or acknowledged Automatic block mode. Set 0, 1, 3, 5, or a custom number of allowed strikes.

Fail-closed safety

Accounts you follow are never blocked. If follow status is unclear, the action stops. X's visible controls are used instead of private APIs.
